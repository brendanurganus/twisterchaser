      Moving the map

      • Move: drag, or use the arrow keys.
      • Zoom: scroll, pinch, double-click, or the + and - keys.
      • Tilt and turn: right-drag or Ctrl-drag, or twist with two fingers. Shift with the arrow keys turns and tilts.
      • Lost? The compass faces north, the corners button fits every tornado.
      • Warnings: the triangle button (or the switch under Filters) shows tornado warning areas. A selected tornado always shows its own.
      • Search: press / to jump to the search box.
